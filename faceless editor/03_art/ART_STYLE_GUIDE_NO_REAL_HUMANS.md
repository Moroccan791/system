# Art Style Guide — No Real Humans (Painterly Only)

**Source reels used:** Painterly illustration (oil texture, muted, grain) — NOT photography. Keep that.

### DO:
- Painterly oil texture, canvas grain 8%, vignette
- Muted dusty palette: beige skin #C8B0A0, charcoal shadow #1A141F, dark guide #0F0A14
- Black void background #000000
- Illustrated figures only (AI generated painterly)
- Eye hold composition variation (A-E)

### DON'T:
- No stock photos, no real people, no photoreal
- No anime / cartoon / 3D / hyper sharp
- No neon / bright colors (except science viz: cyan #00D4FF, gold #D4B483 for data)
