## 2. PHOTO SWAP SYSTEM — How To Look Original

#### Variation C — Age Shift
- **Prompt:** `child and elderly guide, old wise shadow hands holding child's face to see, large expressive eyes, storybook oil painting, soft grain, emotional --ar 9:16`

#### Variation D — No Hands, Same Energy
- **Prompt:** `two figures side by side in darkness, one whispering to the other, closeup faces, one eye glowing faintly, dark academia painterly style, cinematic --ar 9:16`
- **Use when:** You want to avoid “hands on face” entirely but keep intimacy.

#### Variation E — Abstract Guide

**Execution:**
1. Generate 10 images in Midjourney/SD/DALL·E using above prompts (vary seed)

3. **Never reuse the same image twice in a row** — rotate A-E to avoid pattern detection

> **Legal cover:** Ask AI to do “in style of yazed_aljohanii” is risky. Use prompts above instead — they describe *technique* (oil texture, muted, grain) not artist name.
