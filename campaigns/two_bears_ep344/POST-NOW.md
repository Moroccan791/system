## Order, and why

Do **not** post all four in one go — four uploads inside an hour splits your own
reach and reads as a dump.

1. **One in the gym with me** — the strongest open ("one in the gym with me",
   then the selfie, then the door). Lead with the best one; the first post of a
   run is what gets shown to new viewers.
2. **What did you do?** — 4–6 hours later. Broadest one: everybody has been
   Googled by a stranger.
3. **Please, leave. I can smell you from here** — next morning. Best cold open
   line of the four, and it is 20 s, so it is the one a rewatch is cheap on.
4. **Honey, you're fucked** — evening. Shortest, sharpest ending line.

## Same file, four platforms

one of the four platforms' spec. Upload the same file everywhere; there is no
platform-specific cut and making one would only split the views.

## What to watch on the first 24 h

Record views and 3-second retention per post in
[`RESULTS.md`](RESULTS.md). If one post is ahead on retention but behind on
views, that is a distribution problem, not a clip problem; if it is behind on
retention but ahead on views, the hook is doing the work and the clip after it
is not.
