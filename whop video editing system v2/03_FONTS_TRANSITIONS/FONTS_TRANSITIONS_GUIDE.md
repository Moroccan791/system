# 03 — FONTS & TRANSITIONS (Pro, Not Slop)

## Fonts — The 3-Font Rule

### Install Only These 3 (Free, Professional, Covers Every Niche)

| Role | Font | Weight to Use | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DISPLAY (Hook)** | **Anton** or Bebas Neue | Regular 400 | Hooks, big punch words, thumbnails. NEVER body text. |
| **BODY (Captions)** | **Inter** | Bold 700 + Regular 400 | 90% of captions, lower thirds, descriptions. |
| **MONO (Data)** | **JetBrains Mono** | Bold 700 | Numbers, stats, timers, "1,000,000 words", Pi digits. |

**Pairing examples:**
- Existential (Butterfly): Anton hook yellow + Inter body white — what we used for your faceless, looks premium
- Podcast (Sartain): Inter body + JetBrains Mono for "80%" stat — instant credibility
- Finance: Bebas hook + Inter body

**Rules:**
- Never more than 2 fonts on screen at once
- Tracking: Anton -12, Inter -0.5, Mono 0
- Never use: Poppins, Montserrat, Arial, Comic (all scream AI slop / Canva free)

**Where to get:** Google Fonts → download TTF → install → restart Premiere/CapCut

## Transitions — The 90/10 Rule

**AI Slop = transition on every cut.** Pro = 90% hard cuts, 10% intentional motion.

### Allowed (Use these, and ONLY in these spots)

| Transition | Duration | When to use | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hard Cut** | 0f | 90% of cuts. Podcast jump cuts, Sartain answers. Default. | Light pop or nothing |
| **Whip Pan (Directional)** | 8-10f | Energy shift: topic change. Left→right must match camera motion. | Whoosh short |
| **Camera Push (Scale)** | 12f | Punch a stat: 100%→103% scale on "80%". Not on every sentence. | None, let voice punch |
| **Match Cut** | 0f | Visual rhyme: Eye → pi eye → galaxy eye. Your Butterfly uses this eye match perfectly. | None |
| **Cross Fade** | 15f | Only for time jumps: "1,000 years ago" → starfield. Never for dialogue. | Soft wind |

### Banned (Delete from favorites)
- Spin, Zoom Suck, Glitch, Roll, Swirl — instant AI slop tell
- Transition longer than 15f — feels slow, viewer skips
- More than 1 transition per 5 seconds — if you need more, your cuts are bad

### How to Apply Fast (Not Slow)
1. **Edit with hard cuts only** — get story right first. No transitions.
2. Watch once. **Mark 2-3 spots** where energy *needs* movement (e.g., calendar grid reveal, hourglass drop). Add Whip/Camera there only.
3. Done. 2 transitions per 40s video = pro. 10 = slop.

### Sound Design (Makes cheap→pro instantly)
- **Caption pop:** 1 soft click at -18dB on emphasis word only (not every word)
- **B-roll in:** 1 airy whoosh at -22dB on hard cut to stars/calendar (we already generate this in audio drone)
- **Punch stat:** No sound, let YELLOW word + 8% scale do work
- **Never:** Laser, trap hi-hat on captions
