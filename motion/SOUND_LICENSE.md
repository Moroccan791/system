# Sound assets — license

Every sound in the motion pipeline's Stage-C mixes is **synthesised
in code** by `motion/sound.py` from oscillators and filtered noise
( pops, keystrokes, ticks, risers, impacts, sub-bass bed, pad chords).
No audio files are downloaded, sampled, or shipped.

These generated sounds are original works produced by this repository
and are released into the public domain (**CC0**). Fonts and icons are
unchanged from earlier stages (OFL fonts in `assets/fonts/`).
